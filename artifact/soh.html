Where it is

Soh is in the Central District of Shahin Shahr and Meymeh County, on the slopes of the Karkas mountains. It is also written Seh, Sō or Sūh. Neighbours are Abyaneh (north), Bidshak (south), Kalahrud (east), Rebat Aqa Kamal and Meymeh (west).

From the Shahin Shahr to Meymeh road, turn off about 50 km north of Shahin Shahr at Dehlor, then pass Bidshak. Soh is at the end of the road. There is no bus terminal or railway station in the village.

Places to see

Soh Caravanserai کاروانسرای سُه

A stone inscription on the portal dates it to 1223 AH (1808 CE), in the reign of Fath-Ali Shah Qajar, with the builder named as Abolqasem Esfahani. About 3,000 m², four iwans and 20 rooms around a courtyard. National monument no. 7641 (registered 17 Esfand 1381). Restored 1395 to 1400 with local donations.

reportedKojaro

Imamzadeh Yusuf امامزاده یوسف

Shrine in the upper village, about 400 years old, with a 16 m dome in turquoise tile. Said to be the resting place of Yusuf, son of Imam Hadi.

reportedKojaro

Imamzadeh Golileh امامزاده گلیله

Turquoise-domed shrine in the lower village, at its centre.

reportedKojaro

The old bath حمام قدیمی

A Qajar-era bath about 200 years old. Of the upper and lower baths, one survives, restored with local help.

Land and livelihood

About 86% of residents worked in agriculture in a survey from 2007 to 2008: crops 56%, livestock 27%, beekeeping 2.4%, poultry 0.8%. Homes are spread out, far from the fields and orchards. Water comes mainly from surface streams. In wetter years lentils, chickpeas, beans and saffron are also mentioned (FA Wikipedia).

The apples are described as among the best in Isfahan province, and about 30% of the village's economic capacity is apple growing.

Muharram traditions

reportedOn Tasua, during Muharram, the village carries a palm-shaped standard (nakhl-gardani). It is decorated in the morning at Imamzadeh Yusuf, carried to Imamzadeh Golileh, and returned after the Ashura noon mourning. Other rituals include alam-gardani, ammar-gardani, taziyeh and nowheh chanting. Kojaro says these mourning rituals are about 200 years old and were listed in Iran's national intangible heritage (no. 1207) on 5 Mordad 1395. The amari (a small wooden bier-frame, kept by one family) is carried on the 8th; seven standards (five coloured, two black) lead the palm on Tasua; chest-beating and dirges start on the first night (Mehr News, single source). Local foods: kalleh-joosh, kashk-e mama, tanoori abgoosht, golmisheh, shami, semnu.

Seyed Ali Safavi Sohi سید علی صفوی سُهی

Born 29 July 1940; commanded the 293rd tank battalion; killed by shrapnel 2 August 1983 (place differs by source: Fakkeh or Jangal-e Amqar). Father Seyed Musa Safavi. A Tehran bridge carries his name. Whether he is from this Soh is medium confidence (FA Wikipedia, Khabar Foori Mar 2021).

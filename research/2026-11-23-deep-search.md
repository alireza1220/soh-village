## Identity
- The village is Soh (سه / سُه; also Seh, Sō, Sūh), Murcheh Khvort Rural District, Central District, Shahin Shahr and Meymeh County, Isfahan. Coordinates 33.439 N, 51.452 E (FA Wikipedia, Kojaro, OSM). English Wikipedia (31.78 N, 51.37 E) and Wikidata (33.48 N) are wrong or off.
- Not to be confused with Sohr, Sohra or other villages called Soh/Seh.

## Used on the site
- Muharram rites (Mehr News, 30 Tir 1402 / 21 Jul 2023): about 200 years old; amari on the 8th (small wooden frame, hereditary family custody); nakhl decorated at Imamzadeh Yusuf and carried about 2.5 km to Golileh on Tasua; seven standards; chest-beating from night 1; visitors from across the province. Kojaro adds intangible heritage no. 1207 (5 Mordad 1395), not confirmed elsewhere.
- Caravanserai: four iwans, four round corner towers, 20 rooms, about 3,000 m², builder Abolqasem Esfahani, 1223 AH = 1187 solar = 1808 CE, restored 1395-1400. Registry no. 7641 appears only on Kojaro.
- Forts: Bala, Pain, Miyan (Qal'eh Miyan), Chal. More sites: Abbasi caravanserai at Aqa Kamal Pain, three mills, "English telegraph house", Farangi mansion, covered passage, Kandeh Baghestan (Kojaro, 2021 blog).
- Dialect: Iranica "Kashan ix. The Median dialects of Kashan" lists Soh (Sohi / Soʾi); data from Andreas and from Mann and Hadank; Morgenstierne groups Soh with Qoh, Keš and Mey. No verified word list found online.
- Census 2006 403/141, 2011 281/108, 2016 295/127. Murcheh Khvort Rural District 2016: 39 villages, 2,198 people, 758 households; Murcheh Khvort village 1,369/394 (EN Wikipedia).
- Tourism: Kojaro says no lodging in Soh; county inventory Dec 2022 (4 eco-lodges, 1 traveller's house, 1 hotel, 2 guest houses, 3 camping sites; Soh and Kalahrud tourism target villages).
- Banks: Bank Saderat branch, Murcheh Khvort industrial town (Balad: Sheikh Bahai Blvd, Zakaria St, Abu Rayhan 4; another Balad entry says Abu Rayhan 5). Bank Day ATM at Fatemiyeh Sq, Shahin Shahr.
- Property law: changing the use of farmland and orchards outside towns is restricted; exceptions via a provincial commission (ekhtebar.ir law text). The reported 80% fee and the 500 m² personal-home exemption were not published (not verified).
- Media: Instagram @soh_village (active daily, Nov 2026); Telegram t.me/esfahansohi (about 850 subscribers); IRIB News 3114741 (3 Khordad 1400, not read); Gardeshnama; Beinabein (2 Esfand 1400); a 2021 blog with a heritage researcher's view.
- Person: Seyed Ali Safavi Sohi (b. 7 Mordad 1319, killed 11 Mordad 1362; place of death differs by source).

## Conflicts
- Seasonal population: about 1,000 (Kojaro) vs up to 5,000 (Gardeshnama).
- Distance: Kojaro says 15 km east of Meymeh, 75 km from Shahin Shahr and Meymeh; Mehr and a blog say 65 km. Isfahan: 94 km (FA Wikipedia) vs 100 km (Kojaro).
- Caravanserai style: Fath-Ali Shah inscription, but several writers see Safavid style.
- Saray-e Moallem eco-lodge: in Tarq Rud (Natanz County), not near Soh (earlier site text implied otherwise; fixed).
